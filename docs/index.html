The problem

Software limits are optional. That's the whole issue.

Screen time settings live inside the same device you're trying to put down. Three taps and a PIN, and the limit is gone. The friction was never real — it was just another menu.

Digital limit

Turn it off in the settings app

Screen Time, Digital Wellbeing, third-party blockers — all live on the device they're supposed to restrict. Removing them costs a few taps and a moment of willpower you were already low on.

Physical friction

Get up and find the puck

Anchor's lock only lifts at the object itself. Getting around it means physically standing up, walking to where you left it, and tapping — a real pause, not a dialog box.

How it works

Three taps. One object. No account required.

01

Tap to lock

Hold your phone to the puck to start a focus session. The apps you've chosen go dark immediately.

02

Walk away

The puck stays wherever you left it — another room, a locker, a friend's desk. The lock travels with the object, not the app.

03

Tap to unlock

Only the paired puck's unique chip ID lifts the block. Any other tag, or no tag at all, does nothing.

What's inside

A parametric design, not a fixed product.

Every dimension lives in an OpenSCAD file. Change the diameter, the fit tolerance, the mount — it's yours to tune for your printer.
